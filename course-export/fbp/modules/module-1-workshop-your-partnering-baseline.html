Apply Module 1 to your own role. This is the work that turns theory into practice — treat it like a live engagement, not homework. Use the three Module 1 tools (stakeholder map, driver tree, partnering contract) as your worksheets.

Steps

  1. Sketch your organisation's business model on one page: who it serves, what it sells, where the margin is made — with real proportions.
  2. Draw a driver tree from profit (or your unit's key result) down to 4–6 operational drivers, with a rough sensitivity per branch.
  3. Map your top 8 stakeholders on the influence × impact grid and mark the relationship strength of each (strong / working / absent).
  4. Run the three-bucket calendar audit on last month (production / commentary / partnering, in hours) and write your kill list.
  5. Pick the one high-influence stakeholder with the weakest relationship and book 30 minutes with them this week. Open with: 'What does finance do that helps you least?'
  6. Write a two-sentence 'finance value statement': what your partnership offers that stakeholder, in their language.

Deliverable

Record your finance value statement, your calendar split (e.g. 70/20/10), and the stakeholder you chose below. You'll build on all three in the capstone.
