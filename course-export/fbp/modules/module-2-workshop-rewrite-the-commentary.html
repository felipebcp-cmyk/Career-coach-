Take a real (or realistic) piece of monthly variance commentary from your world and rebuild it as insight.

Steps

  1. Pick one month's commentary or a recent analysis you produced (or write a typical 'revenue up X%, costs down Y%' example).
  2. For each point, ask 'so what?' until you reach something a decision-maker cares about, then 'now what?' until you have a recommendation. Cut anything that survives neither.
  3. Restructure top-down: one headline sentence, up to three supporting points, one recommendation.
  4. Rewrite it for a non-finance operations leader: no undefined jargon, one concrete translation of the biggest number.
  5. Sketch (or describe) the single chart that would carry the headline, with the message as its title.

Deliverable

Paste your rewritten headline and recommendation below — one sentence each.
