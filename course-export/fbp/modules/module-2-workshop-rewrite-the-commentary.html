Take a real (or realistic) piece of monthly variance commentary from your world and rebuild it as insight. Use the commentary template and chart checklist from this module's toolkit.

Steps

  1. Pick one month's commentary or a recent analysis you produced (or write a typical 'revenue up X%, costs down Y%' example).
  2. For each point, ask 'so what?' until you reach something a decision-maker cares about, then 'now what?' until you have a recommendation. Cut anything that survives neither.
  3. Restructure top-down: one headline sentence, up to three supporting points, one recommendation — under 120 words.
  4. Rewrite it for a non-finance operations leader: no undefined jargon, one concrete translation of the biggest number.
  5. Redesign (or describe) the single chart that would carry the headline, with the message as its title.
  6. Write the half-page pre-wire plan for the recommendation it contains: who hears it, in what order, in what currency.

Deliverable

Paste your rewritten headline and recommendation below — one sentence each — plus the first name on your pre-wire sequence and why they're first.
