Weeks 1–2

What a finance business partner actually does, the mindset shift from scorekeeper to strategist, how to understand the business and its stakeholders well enough to add value beyond the numbers — and how to redesign your own working week so partnering actually fits in it.

In this module

  1. What is a finance business partner? (~15 min)
  2. The business partner mindset (~15 min)
  3. Knowing the business: models, drivers and KPIs (~15 min)
  4. Stakeholders, trust and relationships (~15 min)
  5. The operating model: buying back the hours (~15 min)

The module closes with an applied workshop and a knowledge check (10 questions, 80% to pass).
