Weeks 1–2

What a finance business partner actually does, the mindset shift from scorekeeper to strategist, and how to understand the business and its stakeholders well enough to add value beyond the numbers.

In this module

  1. What is a finance business partner? (~20 min)
  2. The business partner mindset (~20 min)
  3. Knowing the business: models, drivers and KPIs (~25 min)
  4. Stakeholders, trust and relationships (~25 min)

The module closes with an applied workshop and a knowledge check (8 questions, 80% to pass).
