Weeks 3–4

Turning analysis into narratives that drive action — and building the influence to make them land: the insight ladder, storytelling structure, adapting to different audiences and styles, making numbers visible, and influencing without authority.

In this module

  1. The insight ladder: so what, now what (~15 min)
  2. Storytelling with numbers (~15 min)
  3. Communicating with non-finance audiences (~15 min)
  4. Making numbers visible (~15 min)
  5. Influencing without authority (~15 min)

The module closes with an applied workshop and a knowledge check (10 questions, 80% to pass).
