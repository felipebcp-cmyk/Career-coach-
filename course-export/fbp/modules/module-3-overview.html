Weeks 5–6

Bringing the strategic lens to partnering: human-centred problem solving, confident recommendations under uncertainty, forecasts and scenarios the business actually uses, and the hard conversations that come with the trusted-advisor seat.

In this module

  1. The strategic lens (~15 min)
  2. Human-centred problem solving (~15 min)
  3. Confident recommendations under uncertainty (~15 min)
  4. Hard conversations and guiding through uncertainty (~15 min)
  5. Forecasts and scenarios people actually use (~15 min)

The module closes with an applied workshop and a knowledge check (10 questions, 80% to pass).
