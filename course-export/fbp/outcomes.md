# Learning outcomes — The Finance Business Partner's Playbook

Mapped to skill areas of the CGMA Competency Framework (AICPA & CIMA) and the IMA Management Accounting Competency Framework. Each outcome names where it is taught (L = lesson, W = workshop, Q = quiz, C = capstone) so a marker can verify it.

- **Build a one-page business-model map and a quantified driver tree for your own unit, pairing each major financial line with its two best leading indicators.**
  - Where: L1.3 · W1 · Q1 · Competency: CGMA: Business acumen / IMA: Business acumen & operations
- **Map your stakeholders on an influence × impact grid, assess each relationship with the trust equation, and contract a working relationship with the weakest key partner.**
  - Where: L1.4 · W1 · Q1 · Competency: CGMA: People skills
- **Redesign your working month (kill / shrink / automate) to shift measurable hours from production to partnering, and score the function on decision participation, forecast reliability and stakeholder verdict.**
  - Where: L1.5 · W1 · Q1 · Competency: IMA: Leadership / CGMA: Business acumen
- **Rewrite a monthly commentary as an answer-first decision narrative — one headline, up to three supports, a recommendation — in under 120 words, adapted to a non-finance audience.**
  - Where: L2.1–2.3 · W2 · Q2 · Competency: CGMA: Communication & influence
- **Design a message-titled, decluttered chart that carries one decision message without its author present, within stated graphical-integrity rules.**
  - Where: L2.4 · W2 · Q2 · Competency: IMA: Technology & analytics (data visualisation)
- **Plan and execute a pre-wire sequence (allies → undecided → opponents, framed in each stakeholder's currency) for a live recommendation.**
  - Where: L2.5 · W2 · C · Competency: CGMA: Influence / People skills
- **Construct a one-page recommendation with options including a priced do-nothing, pre-agreed criteria, pre-mortem risks with mitigations, and a stated 'what would change our mind'.**
  - Where: L3.2–3.3 · W3 · C · Competency: CGMA: Decision making / IMA: Strategic management
- **Build a trigger-based scenario set (two dominant uncertainties, three quantified stories, pre-agreed owned responses) and a rolling driver-based forecast with a published bias scorecard.**
  - Where: L3.5 · W3 · Q3 · Competency: IMA: Planning & analysis / CGMA: Business acumen
