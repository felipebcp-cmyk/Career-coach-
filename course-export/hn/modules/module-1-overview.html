Weeks 1–2

How the mind actually allocates attention: why stories beat information, the three tensions — survival, identity, progress — that decide what people notice, believe and act on, and the narrative mechanics that make a message carry.

In this module

  1. Why minds run on story (~15 min)
  2. Survival tension: the problem-shaped mind (~15 min)
  3. Identity tension: tribes, status and belonging (~15 min)
  4. Progress tension: the ladder of needs (~15 min)
  5. Narrative mechanics: how stories actually carry (~15 min)

The module closes with an applied workshop and a knowledge check (10 questions, 80% to pass).
