Theory becomes skill only under an audience. Write all five levers on one idea you actually care about — then let reality grade one of them.

Steps

  1. Pick one idea, product or belief you genuinely want more people to get.
  2. Write five short pieces (2–4 sentences each), one per lever: a problem-naming opener, an 'if you're…' mirror, a 'this isn't for…' line, a single-moment transformation scene, and a ridiculous first step.
  3. Read them aloud. Cut every phrase a real person wouldn't say across a table.
  4. Publish or send the strongest one — a post, a message to a friend, an email. Reality is the grader; theory doesn't count.
  5. Record what happened: replies, silence, shares, pushback. Silence is data too — usually about the mirror being angled at no one.

Deliverable

Paste your strongest lever piece below, name the lever and tension it pulls, and note what happened when a real human met it.
