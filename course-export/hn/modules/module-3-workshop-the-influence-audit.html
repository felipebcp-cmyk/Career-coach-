Take one real, upcoming ask in your life and design it deliberately — tensions, levers, principles, ethics and all. Use the transparency checklist and experiment log from this module's toolkit.

Steps

  1. Choose a real ask with stakes: a job application, a pitch, a raise conversation, a launch post, a difficult favour.
  2. Diagnose the audience: which tension is active for them right now, and which room of awareness are they in?
  3. Draft the ask using at least three levers — name their problem, mirror who they are, and give a first step so small that 'yes' is easy.
  4. Annotate your draft against the six classic principles: which are in play, and is the fact under each checkable?
  5. Run the transparency test on your draft at component level: if they saw every move, would they thank you? Rewrite anything that fails.
  6. Deliver it in the real world within seven days, log the result in your experiment log, and note which lever you'd adjust.
