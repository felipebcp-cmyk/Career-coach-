Weeks 3–4

The working toolkit: five moves that convert the three tensions into attention and action — naming the problem, mirroring identity, drawing the line, painting the transformation, and giving the first step.

In this module

  1. Lever 1 — Name the problem (and meet awareness where it is) (~25 min)
  2. Lever 2 — Mirror the identity (~20 min)
  3. Lever 3 — Draw the line: exclusion and belonging (~20 min)
  4. Lever 4 — Paint the transformation (~25 min)
  5. Lever 5 — Give the first step (~20 min)

The module closes with an applied workshop and a knowledge check (10 questions, 80% to pass).
