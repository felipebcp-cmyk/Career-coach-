The uncomfortable truth

Talented. Hardworking. Invisible.

The market never tells you your work is bad. It does something crueller: nothing. No replies, no sales, no callbacks — while worse work, wrapped around better psychology, wins.

The curriculum

Three tensions. Five levers. One honest operator.

Every lesson: core teaching, a worked example, a "where this goes wrong" list, a do-it-now exercise and named sources. Plus a field workshop every module that puts the ideas in front of real humans who are free to ignore you, and three downloadable tools per module.

How the mind actually allocates attention — and the three tensions that decide what people notice, believe and act on.

  • 1.1 Why minds run on story
  • 1.2 Survival tension: the problem-shaped mind
  • 1.3 Identity tension: tribes, status and belonging
  • 1.4 Progress tension: the ladder of needs
  • 1.5 Narrative mechanics: how stories actually carry
Workshop 1 — Tension spotting in the wild: tag the tensions in five things that stopped you this week, then profile which one owns you. After this, you can't unsee them.

The working toolkit: five moves that convert tension into attention and action, in writing, speaking and product.

  • 2.1 Name the problem — and the five rooms of awareness
  • 2.2 Mirror the identity
  • 2.3 Draw the line: exclusion and belonging
  • 2.4 Paint the transformation
  • 2.5 Give the first step
Workshop 2 — Write the five levers: five short pieces on one idea you care about — then publish the strongest and let reality grade it. Silence is data too.

Where the levers meet the real world — and the line you'll never need to cross.

  • 3.1 The ethics line: persuasion vs manipulation
  • 3.2 Test against reality
  • 3.3 Reading people one-to-one
  • 3.4 The meta-skill: compounding human nature into everything
  • 3.5 The six classic principles — and when each tips into manipulation
Workshop 3 — The influence audit: take one real upcoming ask — a pitch, an application, a raise — design it with the full toolkit, transparency-test it, and deliver it within seven days.
